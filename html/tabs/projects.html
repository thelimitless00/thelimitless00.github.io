ModuPet

Welcome to my first big code project: ModuPet!

As a kid, I loved to collect VPets. I still have my large collection with me.

My biggest frustration is that there was never any perfect virtual pet program. They all had their quirks or other limitations that just became frustrating over time.

My goal with ModuPet is to create a virtual pet program that anyone can enjoy on any modern device. With complete customizability, care options, difficulty settings for casual or experienced care, and more. I'm even working on designing a complete module system where anyone can make any pets or games they want inside ModuPet - hence the name!

ModuPet is still nowhere near done, but progress is being made! And I hope one day it can become a program that people can enjoy, anytime, anywhere.

You can eventually test ModuPet yourself, if you like! I'll be putting releases on my GitHub that you can download and run... eventually.
